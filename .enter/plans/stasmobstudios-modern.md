# StasMobStudios — modern rebuild

A new, modern version of the studio's site, built from the current stasmobstudios.com content, with the recent work featured and room for the new projects.

## Where the content comes from

I read the live site directly: the 17 real projects (titles, descriptions, clients, dates, tags, links), the seven people with their roles and skills, the studio history since 2011, awards/competency list, and the contact details (email, phone, Vilnius address, Skype). I also confirmed the site's project and team photographs are downloadable, so the new site can use the studio's own imagery instead of stock or generated pictures.

The GitHub repository `stasmobstudios.com` on branch `sms` is **not reachable**: it does not exist under the connected GitHub account (a search of all your repositories returns 9 unrelated ones, and the public URL 404s), so nothing from the repository is imported. The rebuild is based on the published website.

## Look

A dark editorial studio direction: near-black canvas, warm off-white type, one muted copper accent, oversized headlines, generous spacing, and project imagery carrying the page. Cards and panels are understated; motion is limited to subtle reveals and hover transitions rather than gimmicks. The whole site is responsive and readable on small screens.

## Pages

- **Home** — hero with the studio's positioning line, a curated row of the strongest work, the new projects, a compact services/capabilities block, a short studio story, the team, and a contact strip.
- **Work** — full archive of all 17 existing projects with category filtering (apps, web, museums, creative), dates and clients intact. Featured work is visually distinguished from the archive.
- **Project pages** — each project gets its own page with real description, client, date, tags, external link where one exists, and its gallery images.
- **Studio** — history since 2011, the team with roles and skills, competency areas, awards/certificates section.
- **Contact** — email, phone, address, Skype, and a message form that opens the visitor's mail client with the message prefilled. It states plainly that it composes an email; nothing pretends to have been sent through the site.

## Featured selection

The curated set on the homepage: TrashSignal, Pray on Mandala, Solveiga Jaskunas, Tuskulėnai Museum (Homo Sovieticus), Trakai Castle, Whispers, RunwayHD and Medinė raidė. The remaining nine stay available in the Work archive.

Until then no empty or fake cards appear.

## Assumptions

- English only; the old site's blog page is not carried over.
- The studio's existing copy is lightly edited for length and clarity, but facts — dates, clients, people, contact details — stay as published.
- Imagery is the studio's own project/team photography, optimised for the web; no stock photography.
